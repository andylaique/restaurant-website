The Ankole Grill Story

The inspiration for the name Ankole Grill comes from the recognition and fascination that the oldest ancestor to all African cattle is the majestic and iconic Ankole Watusi bull.
In its beauty and magnificence, it embodies the love and devotion and deep sense of connection that the Nilotic peoples of East and Central Africa share with their cattle.
It is from this devotion and passion that we find inspiration to invite you to a warm, authentic, and generous dining experience………in the spirit of
true African hospitality.
